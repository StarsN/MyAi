Ser appreciate your question and love the discussion.

My take of "great memecoin = political movement" is pureply from a perspective of "how to recreate a great memecoin," so the missing word before political movement is "perceived" - ppl who felt like they are amongst a brewing political movement, whether it's real or not is unimportant

In terms of how to define the "oppressed" group, it's the retailers. Many of them share the same “Tradfi VS them" "Whales VS them" "VCs VS them" thinking mode (In Chinese “evil market manipulator VS people (狗庄VS散户)". So in that matter, yes many memecoin traders are those who perceived being marginalized and strongly believe them won't find a good trading opportunity in "value tokens".

My point is, to potentially recreate or even massive produce great memecoins, we need to first understand how this rally cry pattern works, and there goes my analogy between political movement & memecoin
