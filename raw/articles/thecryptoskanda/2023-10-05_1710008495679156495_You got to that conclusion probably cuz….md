You got to that conclusion probably cuz you never lived in Australia.

Imagine in a world where you have to fly at least 9 hours to get to a meaningful major city, paying about half of your income as tax, nearly impossible for you to recruit a capable and passionate enough dev team (even if you do it costs you 3x more than overseas), and having a nanny state ALP government in power, I don’t know how in the world it could be a “finance, tech crypto and more” hub 

Singapore is a hub because it’s the gateway to SEA. Hong Kong was a hub cuz it’s the gateway to China. London, Tokyo etc are capitals of a significant industrial/tech power. Sydney & Melbourne came nowhere near unfortunately
