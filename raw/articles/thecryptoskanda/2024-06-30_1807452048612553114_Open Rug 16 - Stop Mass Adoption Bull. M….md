Open Rug 16 - Stop Mass Adoption Bull*. Mass Admission Instead

We have gotten way too used to blaming the industry for its failure to attract Web2 users when the market is down, in the name of "Mass Adoption," which involves building "Web3" products catering to the non-money-making-oriented Web2 users

But this is just illogical non-sense, as cringy as your mom blaming you for kids starving in Africa because you didn't finish your meal

Mass Adoption was never, ever, the cure 👇

🤷‍♂️ Mass Adoption - Who Are The Mass?

So who are the "Mass"?

Don't just vaguely say "Web2 users"

The "Mass" is a diverse group of individuals that can be categorized by their needs

Blind people don't drive Tesla, not because they don't care about the environment, but more likely because they don't have drivers' license - they simply don't have the need. 

Anything that can consistently attract users does so because it meets an irreplaceable need with an irreplaceable solution. The same for crypto

🔍 Find The Relevant Audience of Crypto
Crypto has its boundaries. Every one of its value proposition: privacy, decentralization...stem from its core value: permissionlessness - Something most people can't afford.

Permissionlessness is mainly for those who want to counteract restrictions imposed by banks, governments, and big corporations: 

banks that restrict your account openings and payments, governments that restrict your investments and gambling, and big companies and governments that limit your speech. 

A big F*k You to their faces.

Permissionlessness enjoyers ARE the main audience of crypto. They are not the majority of the world as the majority have been "domesticated" by the system. 

They are the “outliers” of the society who understand arbitrage and are anti-establishment - the "Merchants". You can find them among the self-employed, under-employed, college students, young professionals etc.

These people "speak business." If getting a stable job is like selling a person in a "wholesale manner" to an employer, then Crypto's audience is the kind who want to price every bit of their time, energy, and connections and sell "themselves" at a good price.

For these people, making money as the primary goal is inevitable.

😂Crypto Might Have Already Been Massively Adopted?

So how many of these "Merchants" don't even know crypto yet?

According to the UN, the average annual income for adults globally is $23,000. If they put 10% into Crypto, that’s $18.4 trillion from the 8 billion people worldwide. Based on Binance's 2024 Mar spot trading volume of $112 billion, the annual volume for spot trading alone is over $13 trillion. That leaves just over 30% room for growth. Taking other exchanges into account, this goal might already be surpassed

If we only look at the top earners, top 10% of the world, who account for 52% of global income, then we are talking about 800m people. https://t.co/ZzfFa51NbZ says there were 520 million crypto users worldwide in 2023, leaving roughly 30% room for growth.

Not much left isn't it?

Your impression that there are no new audience is simply because the market is nearing its practical TAM upper limit. It’s rare for an educated person in 2024 not to have heard of Bitcoin

Still think you can "Mass Adopt" Web2 users? 😂 Anyone adoptable has likely already did

♠️ No New Audience? No Problem. Frequency Is The Real Issue

I worked in gambling industry, one of the largest globally. From an industry-wide perspective, there is no such thing as new audience in gambling - heoretically, every wealthy person is our market because they all have a gambling instinct regardless of background. Generally speaking, the gambling industry's growth keeps pace with global inflation

Offline gambling has regional constraints, but Crypto is global, unaffected by regional tourism. If business isn't doing well, it’s not due to a lack of new gamblers but the existing ones are betting less in volume and frequency

🤜The same logic applies to crypto: you don't need tourists, you need the "Merchants" to be your old folks sitting in front of slots all day (high frequency), or you need to fly in VIPs in helicopters (high net worth)

PVP is never a problem. Not PVP frequent enough is.

🧘‍♂️ Mass Admission, Not Adoption

How many of you:
- understand how scoring works in slot?
- can play baccarat?
- Texas Hold'em?

Learning these just takes as much if not more effort than crypto. Why we never heard of "adoption" issue from casinos? Because they meet an irreplaceable need (gambling) with an irreplaceable solution.

They made it super clear: You are here to win. Nothing else. You are "Admitted" into casino, and you have to learn the games and rules. The casino would never cater to your need as other identities to seek "Adoption"

Hard to imagine a VIP club would ever say, "We want to build a social network for gamblers so they spend more in our whisky bar rather than coming here to win money." - All this industry improved is just gone from offline to online, accepting multiple payment methods, improving UX. That's it.

If "Merchants" the main audience want "making money", then crypto should stop pretending to be otherwise

Let people make their bucks! 

Admit them, let them learn the rules of crypto: trading, staking, hedging, lending... Tourists who don't follow or simply suck will be wiped out, and there will be survivors and winners

Your "buidling" needs to be Merchant-centric, not some imaginary "problems" VC bros told you to solve: how to

- admit more people
- admit faster
- make people bid more
- keep people longer
- make the winners tip or show off
- any other relevant needs for a "Merchant"

Education, shorter onramp/offramp, tools, bots, dashboard... UX over stacks. This is not just a philosophy for choosing what project to build, but also for marketing & GTM

Conclusion
Mass Admission is my core logic for evaluating and incubating projects. Think about why people have been bullish on memes, inscriptions, and Solana, but not VC coins, ETH L2s, non-financial applications?
