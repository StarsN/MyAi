A Great Meme = A Political Movement

TL/DR:
Memes are mediums of contagious emotion. However, many of us may not realize that creating a great meme requires more than just finding a viral medium. A better analogy is that a great meme resembles a microscopic political movement.

I will explain the idea with a story:

In 2013, a struggling foreign student, tired of part-time work on minimum wage carrying frozen seafood, began a hustle. This business involved short-term rentals (STR): arbitraging between long-term rental price & accommodation offerings

Soon he realized that the most profitable way wasn't subletting a 2 bedder to 4 Asian kids, but taking newly built condos in upper north bordering the national park. The rental price was surprisingly low due to oversupply but hotel supply is extremely scarce. So he rented to weekend hikers, and secure a 50% occupancy rate & 220% annual return - Super lucrative for a college kid

Back then, Airbnb was a new comer and wasn't big. They were eager to onboard this dude as a VIP host with his whooping 50+ listings, alongside 4000+ hosts in the city.

Soon governments around the world became unhappy about the emergence of so-called 'sharing economy': Uber, Airbnb, you name it, and started acting up. Authorities threatened to crack down local hosts to fulfill their left-winged claim of housing inequality.

Of course our main character was pissed, so he fought back in his own way. Together with local Airbnb community, he mobilized a campaign to advocate the legalization of STR, not in one city but in the whole country: writing to PM, organizing community gatherings, fundraising, supporting legal efforts against the Tenant's Union and state government, and even designing decentralized hardware setups for self check-in to 'grayball' the governements.

This campaign eventually turned into a movement involved over 10000 hosts, the Libertarian Party, and many more across 5 years. Cases in states were won one by one. In 2018, STR was legalized in the last state, and the Airbnb legalization movement was considered a success

The student was me, a 20 year-old random Chinese dude

Why This Story?

What I participated in was a small political movement which involves:

- An oppressed group: short-term rental landlords
- A common interest: short-term rental income
- A common demand: legalization of short-term rentals

What led us to overcome the cynical attitude of "minding our own business" and join this struggle?

Primarily, it was the Emotion of questioning "why others had the right to rule over us".

Then, the Shared Interest that made us have to fight

Finally, the Pursuit of Achievement drove us forward, as recognition and power are intangible rewards that money cannot buy

What does this have to do with MEME?

You might have noticed the parallel between memes and political movements: oppressed groups, common interests, and shared demands.

A great Memecoin is itself a political movement, big or small. Memecoin goes beyond having just a "meme" as defined in SCP Foundation series.

A memecoin allows a group of ppl (marginalized, overlooked) participating in to express common demands (ownership sovereignty, gamble without boundary etc.) based on shared interests (making money)

Alpha hunters, KOLs, shillers in general are basically the 'bolsheviks' or 'advocates' delivering elaborated core message(the "meme") to maximise and unify the audience into one interest group. A memecoin is not a product. It's a voice

That's exactly why you may view some memecoins are "more robust" than others: $BITCOIN is fucking autistics taking over, $People got such a fascinating story to tell, but $Silly or $COQ feel less legit as a memecoin, even they are "backed"

The True Value of Memecoin

VC bros who still criticizing memecoins for "not having any value""being toxic to builder culture" have no clue.

They just don't accept their bags are faded: You can do pricing for NVIDIA, Facebook, a new AI startup, anything with an "immediate financial implication",

BUT how you price a social movement: MAGA, M5S, Brexit, Taiwan 519 Green Action? How do you price the Airbnb legalization movement I participated in? That's where memecoins kick in.

Taking this perspective, the high FDV of memecoins would be self explanatory: $BTC might be worth little as a technical micro-innovation, but it's a gazillion dollar worth political movement seeking ultimate civil liberties

Why China Chinese Team Don't Do As Well In Creating Memecoins?

It's not a skill but a culture issue. Many Chinese meme projects, in terms of the meme itself, or execution, even do far better than their Western counterparts - scam or not.

Most China Chinese grow up in a highly hierarchical culture (both domestically and socially) with little to none exposure to political experience. We were told to stay away from politics when we were young.

Consequentially, we not only have few talents, but even fewer audience who understand the assignments in a political movement. They lack the capability to understand, appreciate, and support an intangible cause.

This further explains the massive gap of narrative acceptance between Eastern and Western crypto scene, but that's another story for next time
