I don’t think it’s an education thing. It’s more a paradigm shift that will not stop with crypto

The young generation are born in an era where career stability is a fugazi that they never experienced. Can you imagine a young lad don’t devote more time to climbing the corporate ladder but to becoming a streamer for those who were born before 1990? 

But that’s what happening: if they never experienced stability, they don’t understand or believe in “fundamentals”, which is everything that modern equity market depending on. This is not just about education

Attention economy will replace the fundamental-driven market for the young generation, and I feel like this shift is only beginning
