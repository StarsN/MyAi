Lido创始人：我绝对不知道，我一个LST创始人，怎么把我选到EF去了。所以Vitalik同志同我说话，“中央已经决定了，你来当总书记” 我说另请高明吧，我是再也不是谦虚，我一个搞LST的怎么到EF来了呢？但是V同志讲“大家已经研究决定了”，所以我后来念了两首诗，叫“苟利以太生死以，岂因祸福避趋之”

Lido Founder:
“I absolutely had no idea! Me, a founder of an LST project—how did I end up being picked for the Ethereum Foundation (EF)? So when Comrade Vitalik spoke to me, he said, ‘The central committee has already decided—you’ll be our General Secretary.’

I said, ‘Please, choose someone more capable! This isn’t humility anymore; I’m serious. How can someone who works on LSTs possibly take on such a role at EF?’

But Comrade V replied, ‘The decision has already been made after careful deliberation by everyone.’

So later, I recited two lines of poetry:
‘If it benefits Ethereum, I’ll risk life and death for it;
Why should I avoid hardship or seek comfort?’”
