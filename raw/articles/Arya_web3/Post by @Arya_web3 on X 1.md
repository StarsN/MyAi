The NFT cost price is 10u, the current total NFT supply is 5825 pieces, the first-day dividend is 1305u, each NFT gets a daily dividend of 0.179u, with a monthly yield of 53.7%, and NFTs also have a floor price for selling, so holders won't lose money;

ET42's hook is the deflationary mode, where NFTs can be converted to tokens or vice versa, and each NFT can be redeemed for 10000 tokens;
