Someone in the TradingView community has created a global M2 indicator to compare with BTC's price movements. Historical data shows a strong correlation between global M2 growth and gold trends. For BTC, however, its correlation with M2 appears with a time lag on a quarterly scale. After the launch of the BTC ETF, shifting M2 data 90 days forward significantly improves its fit with BTC's price trend. Based on this model, BTC still has potential upside aligned with the M2 trend.

From this perspective, with implied volatility (IV) currently at low levels, gradually building a position in bullish options for Q3 and Q4 is indeed a solid strategy.
