The cryptocurrency market experienced extreme volatility last week, with Bitcoin sharply dropping to fill the CME gap at the 79,000 level. Yesterday, the president's tweet triggered a rapid surge. On the #OKX chart, it can be observed that since 2024, every time the one-month ATM implied volatility (IV) was around 40%, significant price swings followed.

In the data lab on https://t.co/HsbOA6TQcv, we can see that IV has already rebounded, and the price has recently touched a resistance level. For those holding spot positions, selling a call option could be considered to earn substantial premiums.
