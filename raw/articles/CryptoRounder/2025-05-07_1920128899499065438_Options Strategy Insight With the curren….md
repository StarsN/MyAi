Options Strategy Insight
With the current technical structure pointing to potential bullish continuation, deploying directional call options becomes a strategically attractive play—especially under conditions of low implied volatility (IV).

On https://t.co/HsbOA6TQcv, traders can easily evaluate suitable call options, taking into account the following:

Strike Selection: Choose strikes within or slightly out-of-the-money based on your risk tolerance and conviction in the breakout.

APR Reference: Assess the annualized percentage return (APR) of each option to understand cost efficiency relative to expected move.

Low IV Advantage: When IV is compressed, call options are generally cheaper, offering better reward-to-risk if the trend resumes strongly.
