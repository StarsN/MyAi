We can see the 1M ATM IV of Bitcoin has dropped to a level similar to late February on https://t.co/AbTOZe9byi. The last time the price broke directly below 90k and dropped to under 80k, both IV and spot trading volume were very low before the volatility occurred, and the market had been in a consolidation phase for about three months. Currently, after experiencing significant volatility, the trading volume is not low, with strong support forming around 80k, while heavy resistance is present near 90k.

Therefore, I believe this low IV may not reverse quickly, and there is still a chance of a consolidation period lasting more than a month. Personally, I will prioritize trading strategies that are suitable for a consolidation environment.
