The outlook from early this month still appears to hold some reference value. This round of institutional bull market has seen ETFs absorb a significant amount of capital.

As of July 10, 2025, the total net inflows into U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have exceeded $50 billion since January 2024. Correspondingly, the supply of BTC on exchanges has been steadily declining, suggesting that major players are accumulating positions by continually driving up prices to attract supply.
