After hitting 76,000 last week, BTC did not show a sustained downward trend. With the strong rebound in the U.S. stock market, BTC prices have recovered to around 84,000.

The key point for Bitcoin's performance in April centers on whether the 76,000 level will be effectively broken. Typically, the market performance in the middle of a quarter plays a decisive role in setting the tone for the entire quarter. If BTC fails to effectively break below 76,000 throughout April, the second quarter is likely to show a consolidation pattern.

Going forward, conducting options trading around the 76,000 support level could be a solid strategy. At the same time, using the VPVR’s Point of Control (POC) at 97,000 as the upper limit of a potential rebound is another key level worth watching.
